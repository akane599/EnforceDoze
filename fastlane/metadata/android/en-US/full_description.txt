EnforceDoze requests Android Deep Doze during configured screen-off intervals, with Shizuku as the primary privileged access mode and root as an alternative.

Optional connectivity, app and sensor-access controls save original states before changes and verify restoration. Access failures and pending recovery remain visible. Sensor access restriction and developer sensor privacy are separate controls; neither proves physical sensors are powered off.

Local diagnostics save point-in-time Deep Doze and sensor-state observations. History records monitoring intervals, not continuous Deep Doze residence. No specific battery-saving percentage is promised.

Includes schedules, call/media protection, Doze exemptions, app suspension, notification filtering, advanced tunables, token-protected Tasker broadcasts and light/dark themes. Turning off connectivity can delay messages and calls. Some controls depend on Android/OEM support. Samsung hardware behavior has not been verified for this development build.

Free, open source, no ads or automatic diagnostic uploads. Restore pending device changes before uninstalling or clearing app data.
